Questions

Before you order.

Do I need to download an app?

No. This website works on any phone. Save it to your home screen and it opens like an app.

Which medicines need a prescription?

Medicines marked Rx need a valid prescription from a registered doctor. Send a clear photo and the pharmacist confirms before packing.

How long does delivery take?

About 45 minutes within 6 km during store hours. Orders after 10:00 pm go out first thing the next morning.

How do I pay?

Cash, UPI or card when your order arrives. You pay only after you see the medicines.

What if a medicine is out of stock?

We call you before packing and offer the same salt from another brand, or arrange it within 24 hours.

Can I return a medicine?

Sealed, unexpired items can be returned within 7 days with the bill. Opened strips and fridge items cannot be returned.
